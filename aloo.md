## Projects

### PRANA: Organ Allocation Platform for India
A web platform for organ allocation and transplant coordination in India, modelled on the US UNOS system. Hospitals register donors and recipients, and the platform runs rule-based liver match runs (blood-group compatibility, geographic priority, MELD-Na ranking), with an admin side for oversight and audit logs. Built with a co-collaborator. *In progress.*

`Python` `Django REST Framework` `React` `PostgreSQL`

[Repo](https://github.com/PRANA-Organ-Transplant-System/PRANA)

### AMRIT: Liver Transplant Mortality Risk Prediction
A machine learning model that predicts 90-day survival for liver transplant candidates from lab biomarker trends (bilirubin, creatinine, INR, sodium, albumin) and MELD scores. *In progress.*

`Python` `Machine Learning`

### MLOps Lab
Seven hands-on experiments covering the MLOps workflow: CI with GitHub Actions, Jenkins pipelines, serving models with FastAPI, containerising with Docker and Kubernetes, experiment tracking with MLflow and DagsHub, and data versioning with DVC.

`MLflow` `DVC` `FastAPI` `GitHub Actions` `Jenkins`

### Rubik's Cube Solver with Q-Learning
A reinforcement learning agent that learns to solve a 2×2 Rubik's cube using tabular Q-learning, with no neural networks.

`Python` `Reinforcement Learning`
